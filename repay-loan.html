Follow these simple steps to repay your loan conveniently:

Access Repayment Link:

Start by clicking the repayment link. You can find this link on our website or in the communication sent to your registered email address.

Enter PAN Number:

Once on the repayment page, enter your PAN number. This helps us quickly locate your loan account for repayment.

OTP Verification:

For added security, an OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number. Enter this OTP to proceed to the next step.

Check Loan Status:

After OTP verification, you will be able to check the status of your loan. This includes details such as the outstanding amount, due date, and any other relevant information.

Review Payable Amount:

Confirm the payable amount displayed. If you wish to pay a different amount, you can adjust it at this stage.

Initiate Payment:

Click on the “Pay Now” button to begin the payment process.

Select Payment Option:

You will be directed to a payment gateway where you can choose your preferred payment option. We offer various payment methods, including debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI, and more.

Complete Payment:

Follow the on-screen instructions to complete your payment. Once the transaction is successful, a confirmation message will be sent to you.
