Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to apply for a personal loan?

+

Indian residents aged 21 to 55 with a stable income can explore loan options. Applicants need valid KYC documents, income proof, and an active bank account for verification and disbursal.

How quickly can I receive a personal loan?

+

Timelines depend on your profile and document verification. Since the process is digital, applications are reviewed quickly, and once approved, funds are transferred directly to your bank account.

How is personal loan eligibility assessed?

+

Eligibility is evaluated based on factors like income, job stability, age, banking history, and repayment capacity. This helps match you with suitable loan options based on your profile.

Can I complete the loan application process fully online?

+

Yes, the entire journey—from filling details to document submission and approval—is completed online. There is no need for physical paperwork or branch visits, making the process simple and convenient.

Are interest rates and charges clearly disclosed?

+

Yes, all applicable interest rates, processing fees, and repayment terms are clearly shown before you proceed. This ensures transparency and helps you make an informed decision.

Can I apply for a loan at any time?

+

Yes, you can apply anytime as the process is fully online. This allows you to complete your application and explore options at your convenience without time restrictions.
