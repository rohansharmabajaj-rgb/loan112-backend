Short Term Loan

A short-term loan is a swiftly disbursed financial option designed for urgent needs. It involves a brief repayment period, requiring weekly or monthly installments. These loans offer a lifeline during extreme emergencies, providing rapid access to funds.

Personal Loan

A personal loan is a versatile financial solution tailored to alleviate unplanned expenses. It offers fixed rates, and regular monthly payments, and doesn't require collateral, making it an accessible choice for various situations.
